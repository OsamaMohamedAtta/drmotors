DR.MOTORS may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, analytics, and advertising.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable regulations.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services, including Google Sheets for vehicle data and email services for customer support.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information to third parties. Information may be processed when necessary to operate the website, provide requested services, comply with legal requirements, or protect the service.

Changes
